# American Truck Simulator — Truck Driving & Freight

---

## 🚛 What Is American Truck Simulator?

American Truck Simulator is a truck driving game developed by SCS Software. It puts players behind the wheel of licensed American trucks, with freight contracts that lead across landscapes inspired by the United States. Each journey combines vehicle handling, road navigation, cargo delivery, and decisions about fuel, time, and operating costs.

The driving world features highways, desert roads, mountain passes, city streets, industrial areas, and rural communities. Routes connect delivery locations across varied scenery, creating journeys that range from local assignments to long-distance freight hauls.

Beyond driving, the career system introduces transport business management. Players can begin by completing jobs for established companies, purchase their own trucks, acquire garages, hire drivers, and expand a growing freight operation. Earnings from completed deliveries help fund vehicles and business development.

Truck upgrades, adjustable driving settings, varied cargo types, and support for different control devices allow players to choose between relaxed sightseeing and detailed driving challenges.

## 🚚 Core Game Features

### 🚛 Licensed American Trucks

Drive detailed trucks based on well-known American manufacturers. Vehicles feature distinct cabin layouts, engine options, chassis configurations, and handling characteristics, offering different choices for freight assignments.

The cabin view places the dashboard, mirrors, and road within the driver's field of view. Adjusting the seating position and mirrors helps with traffic awareness, tight turns, and reversing toward loading areas.

### 📦 Freight Contracts & Cargo Delivery

Accept transport jobs involving a range of cargo, from everyday goods to industrial equipment and specialized loads. Different trailers and freight categories add variety to assignments, while delivery conditions encourage careful driving and route planning.

Freight jobs connect warehouses, factories, construction sites, and other commercial destinations. Completing deliveries earns money and supports career development, allowing players to move from hired driving toward truck ownership.

### 🗺️ American Roads & Landscapes

Travel through environments inspired by American geography, including desert highways, forested areas, mountain roads, major cities, and quiet rural communities.

The map expands through regional content, connecting additional states and destinations. Discovering unfamiliar roads and landmarks adds an exploration element to the work of transporting cargo.

### 🔧 Truck Upgrades & Customization

Personalize vehicles with different paint schemes, cabin options, chassis configurations, engines, and accessories. These choices let players shape the appearance and driving characteristics of their trucks.

Operating a vehicle also involves practical expenses, including fuel and repairs. Managing these costs alongside delivery earnings helps maintain a sustainable transport business.

### 🏢 Transport Business Management

The career system extends beyond working as a hired driver. As earnings accumulate, players can purchase trucks, acquire garages, hire drivers, and expand a transport company.

Business decisions involve balancing vehicle purchases, operating expenses, and income from completed jobs. Players who enjoy management mechanics can coordinate fleet growth while continuing to drive their own routes.

## 🎮 Driving & Gameplay Systems

### 🛞 Detailed Vehicle Handling

Driving requires more than steering along a highway. Vehicle weight, braking, transmission settings, trailer length, and road conditions all affect how a truck responds.

Driving assists and simulation settings can be adjusted to suit different experience levels. More detailed controls allow players to manage transmission behavior, engine braking, and careful maneuvers during challenging deliveries.

### 🧭 Navigation & Route Planning

The route adviser helps drivers follow their journeys and identify upcoming roads and destinations. Route planning becomes especially useful when handling long trailers, entering busy urban areas, or navigating winding mountain roads.

Missed turns and difficult approaches can add time to a delivery. Learning road layouts and becoming familiar with the map are important parts of developing confidence behind the wheel.

### 🚛 Trailers & Specialized Freight

Different trailer configurations accommodate different cargo categories. Flatbeds, refrigerated trailers, dump trailers, and specialized equipment create variety across freight assignments.

Long trailers require care during turns, reversing, and parking. Heavier or unusual loads also encourage players to consider braking distance, road layout, and vehicle control before attempting difficult maneuvers.

### 🌄 Exploration & Photo Mode

Scenic drives offer an alternative to focusing entirely on delivery deadlines. Players can discover landmarks, travel through changing landscapes, and capture memorable views with the in-game photo mode.

Virtual road trips make the game appealing to players who enjoy landscape discovery and the atmosphere of long-distance driving alongside its career mechanics.

### 🎮 Control Device Support

The game supports several control methods, including a keyboard, gamepad, and compatible steering wheel equipment. Control settings can be adjusted to match the available hardware and preferred driving style.

A steering wheel adds a hands-on driving experience, while keyboard and gamepad controls offer practical alternatives for desktop play.
